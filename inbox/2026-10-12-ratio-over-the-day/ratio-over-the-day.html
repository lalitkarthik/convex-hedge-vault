Ratio over the day

How the window ratio (gamma loss of the move, divided by theta earned) is spread at each time of day. SPXW same-day straddles on the lab, 1 July 2025 to 7 October 2026, 316 sessions.

What the data shows

  • The ratio is largest in the first 90 minutes and falls through the day. With 30-minute windows the median is 0.97 in the first window and 0.33 in the last stored one. It is not largest in the last hours.
  • Two things cause this. The move part (locked) is largest at the open and about half as large from late morning. The theta earned per window grows all day and about doubles by 15:00.
  • So the bucket is partly a clock. One set of lines is used for the whole day. A first-window ratio falls in bucket 4 in 41% of cases; a 14:35 window does so in 16% of cases.
  • Every time of day is skewed to the right. The mean is 2 to 3.5 times the median, because a few windows have very large ratios.
  • Both halves of the data and both window lengths show the same direction. Use the controls to check.
  • Not covered: the day's last window (15:05 to 15:35) is not stored by the runs this page reads, so the final half hour is unknown.

Notes

  • Ratio = locked ÷ earned for the at-the-money call and put picked at the window's start. Locked = (delta of call + delta of put)² ÷ (2 × total gamma) at the window's end. Earned = the sum, minute by minute, of ½ × IV² × forward² × gamma × one minute.
  • Source: the ratios stored by four lab runs of spxw_0dte_straddle_after_bucket with rule next_window, trade_after any: sab-lines-w30-h1, sab-w30-next-any-h2, sab-lines-w60-h1, sab-w60-next-any-h2.
  • Windows: 09:35 to 15:35 ET. This rule stores a window's ratio only when a trade follows it, so the last window of the day is missing (11 of 12 windows at 30 minutes, 5 of 6 at 60).
  • Dropped: one window in each first-half run had a missing minute of data and is left out. Half days are not in the lab's data for these runs.
  • Bucket lines: 30 minutes 0.108, 0.475, 1.596; 60 minutes 0.135, 0.639, 1.873. They are the 25%, 50% and 75% points of the first-half ratios.
  • Limits: each window has about 155 values per half, so single figures move by chance. No significance test was run.
