## From convex-hedge-strategy@bc7d9a5 (branch `feature/feature-experiments`, local)

- **Bilal's bucket test works as a description and not as a signal.** The Gap of a 30-minute window sorts the seller's return over the same window on days nobody had read (rank correlation 0.53 hedged, 0.23 not hedged). The Gap from the window before does not sort the next one in a way that pays the spread.
- **Pace and the variance ratio are one feature.** One is 1 ÷ the other squared, so they put the same windows in the same buckets.
- **Implied volatility at 9:35 prices the whole day.** Set a window against what the options charged for that window alone and the "realised above implied in the morning" pattern goes. The median window realises 0.6 of what was charged; added up over all windows, 1.0.
- **A filter chosen on 127 days did not survive 122 fresh days.** It beat 99% of random filters on the days it was drawn on and 33% of them on the fresh days. The whole table is in part 2 of the notebooks.
- **Costs decide the every-window strategy.** Up on 62% to 73% of days at the mid, which is close to Rahul's 65 to 70 in 100. It loses with the spread paid.
- **Delta²/gamma over theta is not RV²/IV² window by window** (rank correlation 0.3). It measures the end point. It explains the seller who does not hedge; realised variance explains the one who does.
- **The nights are a third of a week's movement.** A seven-day implied against realised comparison that leaves them out is wrong by nearly a factor of two in variance.
- **Implied volatility rises on down moves and does little on up moves** (slope −3 against about zero). A hedge that allows for it lowered hedged variance by 10% on fresh days.
- **Half days break the repo's clock.** `quotes/loader.py` counts every day to 16:00. 28 November and 24 December 2025 closed at 13:00. Found by the reviewer agent, not by Claude.
- **Writing the claim before opening the data caught Claude's own best lead.** The regime rule looked real on the seen days and failed on the fresh days. Two smaller leads came back only at the edge.
