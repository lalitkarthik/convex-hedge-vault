## Concepts taught

**Step 4 — reading the engine's strategies**, on `nifty-straddle`. The rule below is Claude's
reading of the page's state fields, not of the strategy's code.

- Entry at 09:17: sells 100 lots of the call and 100 of the put at the strike nearest the
  forward. One lot is 65 units (5,65,595.88 ÷ 8,701.65).
- `expected_move` 200.60 is forward × entry IV × √(1 ÷ 252): 22,610.46 × 14.08% × 0.0630.
- `band` 33.43 is the expected move ÷ `band_divisor` 6.
- `ratio` is net delta ÷ net gamma, in index points. When it passes the band the strategy moves
  lots from one leg to the other until the delta is near zero. The total stays 200 lots. The
  10:43 hedge fired at a ratio of 34.28.
- Exit time 15:35.
