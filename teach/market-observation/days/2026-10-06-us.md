## Concepts taught

**Step 6 — intrinsic and time value; why decay speeds up.**

- Price = intrinsic + time value. Intrinsic for a call is forward less strike, floored at zero.
- At 12:11, forward 7,836.62: the 7,835 call cost 8.25, so 1.62 intrinsic and 6.63 time value.
  The 7,840 call cost 5.75, all time value. The 7,800 call cost 37.45, with 0.83 time value,
  which is the price of the 7,800 put (0.825): put-call parity again.
- ATM straddle ≈ 0.8 × level × IV × √(time). Today's expiry at 12:11: 0.8 × 7,836.6 × 4.92% ×
  √(0.587 ÷ 252) = 14.89; the chain gave 14.90. Tomorrow's expiry at 12:14: 36.0 against 35.95.
- Because of the square root, the loss per minute is price ÷ (2 × time left), and it grows as
  time runs out. Half the opening price is left with a quarter of the day to go.
- Checked over 45 minutes. Today's straddle: 14.90 to 12.95, a loss of 13%. Tomorrow's: 35.95 to
  34.75, a loss of 3%. The formula gave 12.90 and 34.76.
- **The page's theta.** *Claude's inference, now fitted on two expiries:* it is what the option
  loses over the next full trading day at an unchanged level and IV. With under a day left that
  is all of the time value (7,835 call: time value 6.63, theta −6.65). For tomorrow's ATM leg:
  17.4 × (1 − √(0.49 ÷ 1.49)) = 7.41; the page read 7.40.

**Step 7 — delta as a label and as a probability.**

- Delta is close to the chance, as the option is priced, that it finishes in the money. On a
  same-day expiry the two differ in the third decimal.
- Checked at 12:11. The 7,850 call is 13.4 points out; one standard deviation at its IV of 4.79%
  is 18.1 points; 0.74 SD out gives a chance of 23.0%. The page's delta: 0.2305. The 7,860 call:
  1.26 SD, 10.4%; the page: 0.1044.
- So 25Δ means 0.67 SD from the forward and 10Δ means 1.28 SD, each with that strike's own IV.
  In implied moves that is about 0.85 and 1.6.
- A delta names the same chance on any day; the strike it names moves. The 25Δ call was 7,849 at
  12:11 and about 7,839 at 12:56, as the index fell 8.7 points and time ran down.
- A sold 25Δ strangle is priced to finish inside its strikes about half the time, a 10Δ one about
  80%. The put strikes sit further out than the call strikes for the same delta; that is given
  here as a number only and is step 8.
