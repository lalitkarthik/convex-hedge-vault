## Concepts taught

**Step 6 — intrinsic and time value; why decay speeds up.**

- Price = intrinsic + time value. Call intrinsic is forward less strike, not below zero.
- At 10:38, forward 22,642: the 22,600 call cost 175.10, so 42 intrinsic and 133 time value. The
  22,650 call cost 146.73, all time value. The 22,400 call cost 307.63 with 66 of time value, the
  price of the 22,400 put (65.98).
- Equal days, unequal losses. A 308-point straddle with 5 days left, level and IV held, is worth
  276, 239, 195, 138 and 0 as the days go. It loses 33, 37, 44, 57 and 138.
- The reason is ATM straddle ≈ 0.8 × level × IV × √(time). Checked: 0.8 × 22,660 × 12.1% ×
  √(4.79 ÷ 252) = 302; the chain gave 299.88.
- Checked over 44 minutes: 299.88 at 10:35 to 295.90 at 11:20. The formula gave 296.25. That is
  1.3% lost; SPXW on its last day lost 13% in the same length of time on 6 October.
- **The page's theta** is the loss over the next full trading day (*Claude's inference*, now fitted
  on four chains). NIFTY's ATM leg: 150 × (1 − √(3.79 ÷ 4.79)) = 16.6; the page read 16.8.

**Step 7 — delta as a label and as a probability.**

- Delta is close to the chance, as the option is priced, that it finishes in the money.
- Checked at 10:38. The 22,900 call is 250 points above the page's forward; one standard
  deviation at its IV of 11.59% is 362 points; 0.69 SD gives 24.5%. The page's delta: 0.2485. The
  23,150 call: 1.36 SD, 8.7%; the page: 0.0907.
- 25Δ is about 0.67 SD from the forward and 10Δ about 1.28 SD. Today that is 22,400 and 22,900,
  about 1.1% away, and 22,100 and 23,150, about 2.3% away.
- The label stays; the strike moves with time left. On NIFTY's expiry day at 11:28 the 25Δ strikes
  were 22,600 and 22,750, about 0.3% away, with 0.65 of a day left.
