10. Rahul's trading explanation
- [clear] Institutions hedge a portfolio (say Rs 1,000 crore) by buying puts and selling calls. When
  the market falls the portfolio is worth less, the hedge is too big, so they exit the puts and buy
  back the calls: the opposite of the textbook, and call premiums rise on a big fall.
- [clear] After the 400-point fall Rahul closed the put ratio from the previous day and put full
  margin into call ratios. If the market stays or reverses slightly, the structure normalises; if
  it falls further he is on the call side and theta-positive; if it reverses he makes money on
  calls. As call IV subsides he cuts calls for profit and deploys into puts. "We are always trading
  non-directional."
